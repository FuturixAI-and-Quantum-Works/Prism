# 61 contract and legal-document templates

Start with one of 61 reusable templates. Prism brings template discovery, guided fields, an editable preview, and document creation into one workflow.

## What you can do

- Search templates by name or description, filter by category, and sort the library.
- Preview a form and fill its fields, with required-field checks before document creation.
- Edit the preview to adapt the draft to your matter.
- Create a document you can continue editing, reviewing, and sharing in Prism.
- Extend a deployment's library with operator-owned or appropriately licensed DOCX templates.

## Bundled library

The bundled library contains **54 DOCX templates and seven HTML templates**. The 54 DOCX files cover:

| Collection                                        | Templates | Examples                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Property, leasing, and powers of attorney         |        20 | Rent agreements, sale deeds, family settlements, mortgages, and property powers of attorney.        |
| Corporate resolutions and meeting notices         |         5 | Board meeting notices, board resolutions, and shareholder resolutions.                              |
| Founders, equity, ESOP, and intellectual property |        14 | Founder agreements, equity splits, ESOP documents, share subscriptions, and IP assignments.         |
| Legal notices and arbitration                     |        15 | Payment recovery, breach of contract, employment notices, IP infringement, and arbitration notices. |

These are inventory groupings; the DOCX pack appears in the application under **FuturixAI Legal Templates**.

## From template to draft

For a consulting engagement:

1. Open **Templates**, search for **Consulting Agreement**, and open its preview.
2. Fill the fields for the engagement and check the required values.
3. Review the preview and make any appropriate changes.
4. Create the document, then use the [document editor](../document-editor/README.md) to continue working on the draft.
5. Use [collaborative review and approvals](../review-and-approvals/README.md) to collect feedback before final use.

## Use your own DOCX templates

The operator import workflow analyzes DOCX placeholders and makes the source available for document creation. Use only files you own or have permission to use and redistribute, and follow the [DOCX import guide](../../docs/template-catalog.md#import-operator-owned-docx-templates).

Configure working document storage before importing. Keep the source template unedited when you need generation from its original DOCX package; manually edited previews generate from HTML, so formatting can differ from the source Word file.

## Before using a template

Templates are starting material, not certified legal forms. Governing-law choices do not localize every clause or establish compliance with that law. A qualified lawyer should review the text, facts, execution requirements, and applicable law before use.

For help adapting a draft and exploring supporting documents, see [AI-powered drafting and document assistance](../ai-drafting/README.md).
